Controller-prompt fragments owned by the recovery plugin (see plugins/prompt_text.py for
the [section] convention; {placeholders} are filled by plugin.py via str.replace).
The note_* sections are the per-event prompt notes the runner carries between steps;
a note expires when the stage completes or a later close measurably HOLDS.

[context]
Recovery: {note}

[note_empty_grasp]
Empty close; do not retry on an edge/corner. Recenter body and confirm depth.

[note_unverified_grasp]
Grasp occupancy is not decided by a universal width band; inspect AgentView/Wrist and
continue only if the target is visibly held. A width near the open reference can be a
thick held object.

[note_lost_grasp]
Grasp lost; the object may have moved, fallen, or come to rest tilted. Return to
APPROACH and reacquire the object's current visual silhouette in AgentView before
choosing another descent. Use fresh target evidence and the robot height to move
horizontally until the open fingers are over the object; do not repeat MV_DOWN just
because the object is lower in the image. Once the object is again centered at a
safe pre-grasp height, decide GRASP from the two live views.

[note_place_miss]
Placement missed while the object is still held; keep the gripper closed and inspect a
fresh AgentView/Wrist pair. Decide from the current image whether to lift clear of a rim,
correct horizontally, or reacquire the object's relation before lowering again. Never
release during this recovery.

[note_unsettled]
The width is near the commanded-open reference; treat occupancy as unknown and let the
Agent use the live views rather than applying a scene-specific holding threshold.
